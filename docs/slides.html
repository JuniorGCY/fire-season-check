Fire Season Check

Two NASA satellites, one common ruler:
is this fire season above or below normal?

For firefighters, brigades and civil defence. Tocantins (Brazil) and the Argentine Chaco.

Sam (PALMVY) · Londrina Local Event juniorgcy.github.io/fire-season-check

The problem

25 years of fire records, split across two sensors that do not agree

MODIS (Terra, Aqua) · since 2000

  • Pixels of about 1 km²
  • The long history managers need
  • Satellites near the end of their lives

VIIRS (S-NPP) · since 2012

  • Pixels of about 0.14 km², sees smaller fires
  • About 4 VIIRS hotspots for each MODIS hotspot in Tocantins
  • Different overpass times

Raw counts cannot be compared, so a fire manager cannot answer a simple question: is this season worse than usual?

The answer first

Tocantins, through November 1, 2026

−41%

hotspots against the average of the previous 10 years: the lowest of 11 years for the same weeks. Against their median: −41% too.

Recomputed four other ways (MODIS alone, VIIRS alone, fire power, one yearly ratio): between −38% and −43%. Argentine Chaco: −61% against the average, −51% against the median, also the lowest of 11 years.

Data: NASA FIRMS, through 2026-11-01. Newest weeks are near-real-time and provisional.

How we harmonize

A measured ruler, tested on years it has never seen

MODIS-equivalent = VIIRS × factor

Method

  • MODIS before 2012, VIIRS × factor after it
  • Factor fitted per calendar month on the years both satellites fly
  • Fit on 2012–2018, test on 2019–2026, and the reverse
  • Series keeps going on VIIRS when MODIS stops

Out-of-sample error per week

  • Tocantins: 9.2% typical, 38.9% in the worst 10% of weeks
  • Argentine Chaco: 9.3% typical, 33.2% worst 10%
  • Shown on the page next to every answer

What we found

There is no single MODIS/VIIRS factor

MODIS hotspots per VIIRS hotspot, measured on 2012–2026.

  • It changes by month, by time of day and by region.
  • A factor copied from one region is about 25% off in the other.
  • Stress test, Paraná Delta (small fires): the tool reported a 23% error and we left it off the page. There, 2026 is −60% against the mean but only −8% against the median: one extreme year can mislead.

The tool

Pick an area and dates, get the fire calendar

See

  • Draw on the map or type latitude/longitude
  • Year × week and year × month calendars, from 2000 on
  • Each week and month against the 10-year average and median
  • Latest month: October 2026 in Tocantins, −51% against its median

Take away

  • CSV of individual hotspots with a harmonized weight
  • Weekly, monthly and per-cell CSVs; calendars and map as PNG
  • Static page: no server, no keys, works on a phone
  • Accessible colours, keyboard use, text alternatives

Honest limits · next steps

What it does not do yet

Limits

  • Two validated regions; the pipeline re-validates any FIRMS box, but that is evidence, not proof
  • One factor for day and night together; VIIRS S-NPP only
  • Hotspots are detections, not confirmed fires; no brigade has field-tested it yet

Next

  • Separate day and night rulers; add NOAA-20/21 VIIRS
  • More regions; median-first headline where the mean misleads
  • Field test with Tocantins brigades
